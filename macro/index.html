Alarms if a size is missing or out of range, then faces to Z0.

%
O8000 (PART FAMILY SUB)
(HAAS)
(CALLED BY O8001)
(B#2  CLEAR DIA OVER DIAMOND POINTS - 118.)
(Q#17 STOCK ON EACH OP1 FACE - 2.)
(X#24 FLAME CUT HOLE DIA - 36.)
(H#11 OP SELECT)
(S#19 START FROM TOOL NO - OPTIONAL)
(--- CHECK ARGUMENTS ---)
IF[#2EQ#0]GOTO901
IF[#11EQ#0]GOTO901
IF[#17EQ#0]GOTO901
IF[#24EQ#0]GOTO901
IF[#11NE1.]GOTO902
IF[#2LE0.]GOTO903
IF[#17LE0.]GOTO903
IF[#24LE0.]GOTO903
IF[#2LE#24]GOTO903
(S DEFAULTS TO 1 IF LEFT OUT)
IF[#19NE#0]GOTO50
#19=1.
N50
IF[#19GT1.]GOTO910
G0 G40 G80 G99
G28 U0.
G53 Z-420.
G50 S1800
G0 T0101
G96 S200 M3
Z50.
G0 X#2
G0 Z[#17+.5] M8
G72 P110 Q120 U0. W.2 D1. F.22
N110 G0 Z0.
N120 G1 X[#24-4.] F.15
G70 P110 Q120
G0 U2.
G0 Z50.
G28 U0. M9
G53 Z-420.
M99
N901 #3000=1(ARGUMENT MISSING)
N902 #3000=2(THIS EXAMPLE ONLY HAS OP H1)
N903 #3000=3(DIA OR STOCK OUT OF RANGE)
N910 #3000=10(START TOOL NO TOO HIGH FOR OP)
%

Example sizes. Loop steps the OD down to the finish diameter.

%
O8003 (WHILE OD ROUGH STEPS)
(HAAS)
(EXAMPLE SIZES - NOT A JOB)

G28 U0.
G53 Z-420.

G0 T0101
G96 S200 M3
Z50.
#1=80. (START DIA)
#2=50. (FINISH DIA)
#4=2. (DOC ON DIA)
#26=15. (LENGTH)
IF[#1EQ#0]GOTO901
IF[#2EQ#0]GOTO901
IF[#4EQ#0]GOTO901
IF[#26EQ#0]GOTO901
IF[#1LE#2]GOTO902
IF[#4LE0.]GOTO902
IF[#26LE0.]GOTO902
#100=#1
WHILE[#100GT#2]DO1
#100=#100-#4
IF[#100GE#2]GOTO60
#100=#2
N60 G0 X[#1+4.]
G0 Z2.
G1 X#100 F.2
G1 Z-#26
G1 X[#1+4.]
G0 Z2.
END1
G0 Z50.
G28 U0. M9
G53 Z-420.
M30
N901 #3000=1(ARGUMENT MISSING)
N902 #3000=2(DIA OR DOC OUT OF RANGE)
