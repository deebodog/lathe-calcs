F–X offset
G–Z offset
On the sheet, F is marked X and G is marked Z.
F = r − r·tan((90° − θ)/2)
G = r − r·tan(θ/2)
θ = slope angle measured from the Z axis (0° = straight turn along Z, 90° = face). Check: θ 45° gives F = G; θ 90° gives F = r, G = 0. F(θ) = G(90° − θ).

Diagram: Z across, X up. Blue line = part slope at θ. The nose radius (r) touches the slope. The red dot is the imaginary tool tip (the sharp corner the nose would have, T3 position). F is the X distance and G is the Z distance from that tip to the slope line. Drawn to the angle you enter, not to scale for r.
