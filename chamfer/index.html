Adam's Chamfer Blend (OD)

Leicester, England · OD only

OD only. This is for a chamfer rolling onto an outside diameter, tool cutting toward the chuck (−Z). On a bore the same blend is G02, not G03. R is the part blend radius. Start from the sharp corner where the chamfer meets the diameter. A = 90° is a square step (a face into the OD).

For manual tool-nose-radius use, the corner Z must be the tip-path corner, not the drawing corner: drawing Z − r·(1 − tan(A/2)). For 45° that shift is 0.586r (e.g. −0.075 − 0.0092 = −0.0842 with r 0.0156). Turn the shift on to enter the drawing corner and r, and the page applies it.
The inch manual example (Load manual example) is the book job: Ø1.200, Zc −0.0842, R 0.0156. The book prints N67 as Z−0.0906, which is 0.0001 off the exact −0.09066 (this page rounds that to Z−0.0907).
